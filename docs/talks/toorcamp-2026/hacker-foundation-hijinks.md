# Hacker Foundation Hijinks

Delivered at ToorCamp 2026 on Thursday, June 25, 2026 at 5:30 PM Pacific in the Prime Dome.

This 20-minute talk tells the story of how the Portland Hacker Foundation was set up to accelerate security research around Portland and create asymmetric impact for the local hacker community. It covers the foundation mechanics, the research it has funded so far, and how other communities can build similar systems.
